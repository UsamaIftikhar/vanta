```text
Demo store URL: https://test1-st8x0er8.myshopify.com
Storefront password: siyubi

VANTA is an Online Store 2.0 footwear theme. No customer account credentials are required.

1. Home page: Review the immersive hero, category cards, dimensional product story, detail gallery, image hotspots, lookbook, featured products, editorial campaign and footer. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
2. Navigation: Open Catalog in the desktop or mobile header to access the merchant's collections. The account component is available in both desktop and mobile navigation.
3. Collection: Open /collections/all to test category navigation, availability/price filters, sorting, pagination, product cards, swatches when product data supplies them, and quick add for single-variant products.
4. Search: Use the header search for predictive results, then submit a query to review the full results and empty state.
5. Product: Open /products/vanta-aero to test variant URLs, price and availability updates, image zoom, quantity, Add to bag, accelerated checkout, pickup output when configured, sticky purchase link and recommendations.
6. Cart: Test the drawer and /cart page, including quantity changes, removal, cart notes, error messaging and checkout handoff.
7. Theme Editor: Header and footer are section groups. Product sections support app blocks and Custom Liquid. Homepage editorial sections can be reordered. Optional 3D sections are poster-first, lazy loaded, and retain a static fallback when WebGL or a model is unavailable.
8. Accessibility/fallbacks: Keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion and JavaScript-disabled navigation, variant links and add-to-cart are supported.

Checkout should be tested only through the demo store's configured Bogus Gateway or Shopify Payments test mode.
```

**What makes your theme unique from others?**

```text
VANTA is an original, editorial-first Shopify theme created for footwear and movement-led fashion brands. Its design system combines oversized typography, disciplined dark-and-acid color direction, immersive campaign layouts, product-detail storytelling, image hotspots, lookbooks and optional poster-first 3D scenes without relying on a page-builder app. Commerce remains central through native variants, predictive search, dynamic collection categories, filtering and sorting, quick add, a cart drawer, accelerated checkout, pickup availability and product recommendations. Motion and 3D are progressively enhanced, respect reduced-motion and data-saver preferences, and always preserve a static, purchasable fallback.
```
